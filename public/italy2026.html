Day3Mon

St. Mark's & The Palaces 🏛️

June 1 · Venice Full Day · Republic Day Holiday
🏨 Alle Guglie Boutique Hotel — Cannaregio
⚠️ Republic Day — National Holiday June 1 is a major Italian holiday. St. Mark's Basilica and Doge's Palace will be busier than usual. Get there EARLY — ideally before 9 AM — to beat the lines and the heat. Book tickets in advance if not already done.
Early Morning — Before 9 AM
Head to St. Mark's Square first thing. The light is stunning in the morning and the crowds haven't arrived. Walk into St. Mark's Basilica (free entry, book a time slot online). The gold mosaics inside are extraordinary.
By 10–11 AM the square is wall-to-wall tourists. Going early means you actually experience it rather than fight through it.
Morning — 9:30 AM onward
Doge's Palace (Palazzo Ducale) — buy skip-the-line tickets. Marvel at the Bridge of Sighs and the massive council chambers. Plan 1.5–2 hours inside.
The Doge's Palace is the crown jewel of Venice's civic architecture. The interior scale genuinely surprises people — it's worth every minute.
Afternoon
Escape the St. Mark's crowds — walk deep into Dorsoduro or cross to the Accademia neighborhood. Browse, find a canal-side café, take a break. Optionally visit the Peggy Guggenheim Collection (modern art, beautiful terrace on the Grand Canal).
Evening — Splurge Dinner
Il Ridotto — Venice's intimate 1 Michelin Star restaurant. Venetian seafood tasting menu. This is the one special dinner in Venice. Reserve well in advance.
You're leaving Venice tomorrow morning. This is your last Venice evening — worth the splurge.
Tonight: Prep for Tomorrow
Pack your bags tonight. You're picking up the rental car at VCE airport tomorrow and driving to the Dolomites. Checkout + airport transfer + 3.5hr drive means an early start.

Day5Wed

Val di Funes — Santa Maddalena + Adolf Munkel Trail ⛪

June 3 · Ortisei Day Trip · Val di Funes
🏨 Classic Hotel am Stetteneck — Ortisei
Morning — Santa Maddalena Viewpoint Hike
Short drive (~25 min) to Val di Funes. Hike to the Santa Maddalena viewpoint — 5.3 km, ~2 hours, easy. The iconic 14th-century church framed by the Odle/Geisler spires behind it — one of the most photographed spots in the Dolomites. Perfect morning light on the peaks.
Val di Funes is a protected nature park. The Santa Maddalena church composition — red-and-white chapel, green meadows, jagged grey spires — is the quintessential Dolomites postcard. Do it in morning light for best colors.
🥾 AllTrails — Santa Maddalena Viewpoint
Late Morning — Drive up to Zannes
Drive 10 min further up the valley to Zannes trailhead. Start the Adolf Munkel Trail — a 9.2 km easy-moderate loop right at the base of the Odle peaks. The trail hugs the cliff face directly beneath the spires — incredibly dramatic.
🥾 AllTrails — Adolf Munkel Trail
Lunch — Geisleralm Hut
Stop at Geisleralm mid-hike for lunch. Order the Kaiserschmarrn — shredded sweet pancakes with powdered sugar and jam. A Dolomite hut classic. The terrace has direct views up to the Odle towers.
Afternoon — Back to Ortisei
Back in Ortisei by mid-afternoon. Browse the pedestrian woodcarving district — Ortisei is the woodcarving capital of the Alps, every shop is hand-carved local work. Pick up a souvenir.
Evening
Dinner at Tubladel — the best restaurant in Ortisei. Order the Schlutzkrapfen or try the seasonal specials. Easy night before the big Seceda gondola day tomorrow.

Walk to the Ortisei gondola station (2 stages: Ortisei → Furnes → Seceda) up to 2,500m. Pre-booked tickets — arrive by 8:15 AM. The two-stage ride takes ~20 min total. Morning light on the Fermeda/Odle spires from the top is extraordinary.

Morning — Seceda–Rifugio Firenze Circuit
Hike the Seceda–Rifugio Firenze–Col Raiser circuit — 4.9 miles, moderate, 3–3.5 hours. The Fermeda/Odle spire ridgeline is one of the most dramatic views in the entire Alps. The trail runs right along the edge with vertical drops on one side and panoramic valley views on the other.
🥾 AllTrails — Seceda Circuit

Stop at Baita Sofie hut on the terrace for coffee and strudel. The view of the Odle spires from the terrace is the money shot — this is the one for photos.
Lunch — Rifugio Firenze
Lunch at Rifugio Firenze mid-hike — speck and cheese platter, local wine, mountain air. One of the best-positioned rifugios in the Dolomites with direct spire views.
Afternoon — Gondola Down + Ortisei
Take the gondola back down (included in round-trip ticket). Rest, gelato, light afternoon in town. You've earned it — this is the crown jewel hike of the Dolomites stay.

June 5 · Ortisei Last Day · Rest & Recovery
🏨 Classic Hotel am Stetteneck — Ortisei (Last Night)

Well-deserved rest day after two days of hiking. Visit the AVALON Wellness Spa at Mountain Spa Resort Hotel Albion — a 2,000m² facility with a heated Alpine Sky Pool overlooking the Dolomites, Finnish saunas, Turkish bath, and hydrotherapy circuits. The pool view alone is worth it.
After Seceda and Val di Funes, your legs will thank you. Alpine spa culture is a legitimate local tradition here — not a tourist thing. The thermal pools with mountain views are one of the great simple pleasures of the Dolomites.
🌐 Hotel Albion — AVALON Spa

Drive from Dobbiaco to Misurina (~20 min), then up the toll road to Rifugio Auronzo at 7,700 ft. Park at the trailhead. The parking fills and weather rolls in by early afternoon — early start is essential.

Morning — Tre Cime Loop
Classic counterclockwise loop around the three iconic spires (Tre Cime di Lavaredo). The trail wraps around all three peaks — each angle reveals a completely different, jaw-dropping view. Plan 4–5 hours at a comfortable pace.
Mid-Loop — Rifugio Locatelli
Stop at Rifugio Locatelli — the best viewpoint on the loop with the peaks reflected in small ponds behind the hut. Order Goulash Soup — the classic hiker's meal. This is the "Castle in the Sky" view that looks like a painting.
Rifugio Locatelli is the iconic Instagram viewpoint of the whole trip. Budget 30–45 min here for photos, soup, and taking it all in.
Optional Add-On — Cadini di Misurina
If energy allows, drive down to Lago di Misurina (stunning milky-blue lake visible on the way) and take the short walk to the Cadini di Misurina viewpoint — another stunning spire cluster, totally different from Tre Cime.

Day11Tue

Ferry Triangle — Bellagio & Menaggio ⛴️

June 9 · Varenna Day Trip by Ferry
🏨 Marble House in Varenna
Morning — Ferry to Bellagio
Take the mid-lake car ferry from Varenna to Bellagio (15–30 min). Bellagio is the most famous Como village — lush gardens, steep alleys, grand villas. Walk up the Via Garibaldi to explore, visit Villa Melzi gardens if open.
Bellagio is worth seeing, but it's touristy. Going in the morning before the day-tripper boats arrive makes it much more enjoyable.
Late Morning — Lunch in Bellagio
Ristorante Bilacus for lake fish and seasonal menu — quiet, refined, excellent. Or grab lighter bites at La Punta with lake views and aperitivo.
Afternoon — Ferry to Menaggio
Short ferry hop across to Menaggio — the least touristed of the three. Walk the lakeside promenade, have a gelato, enjoy the slower pace. Then ferry back to Varenna.
The classic "ferry triangle" between Varenna, Bellagio, and Menaggio uses the same ferry ticket. It's the quintessential Lake Como experience — seeing all three villages in one day from the water.
Evening — Passeggiata in Varenna
Walk the Varenna promenade at golden hour — hands down one of the most beautiful evening walks in Italy. The light on the mountains and lake is exceptional. Dinner at a lakeside trattoria or aperitivo with views.

Morning — Train to Milan
Take an AM train to arrive in Milan by midday. Drop bags at the hotel, then hit the city — you have the whole afternoon.
Afternoon — Duomo Rooftop
Walk to the Duomo di Milano — Italy's largest cathedral. Buy rooftop access tickets on-site or in advance. Walking on the rooftop among the spires with views over the city is surprisingly thrilling. Plan 1.5 hrs total.
The Duomo rooftop is Milan's essential experience. The cathedral exterior is dramatic from street level, but walking on the roof with the marble spires rising around you is genuinely extraordinary.
Late Afternoon — Shopping & Galleria
Walk through the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II — Milan's breathtaking 19th-century shopping arcade. Window shop the luxury brands, have a Campari at one of the café terraces inside. The mosaic floor and glass dome are iconic.
Evening — Final Dinner
Ratanà (Bib Gourmand) — refined modern Milanese, risotto is legendary.
