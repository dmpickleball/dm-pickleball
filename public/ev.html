Results & closing line value
Why CLV is the number that matters. Beating the closing line — consistently getting a better price than where the market settled — is the only reliable evidence of skill over a large sample. A positive bankroll over a handful of bets is mostly variance; it does not prove an edge. Aim to judge yourself on average CLV across many bets, not on short-run profit.
