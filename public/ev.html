Fair %The "true" probability after removing the house's cut. If Fair % is 52%, the market thinks this team has a 52% chance of winning.
Kalshi ¢What you pay per $1 to bet on this outcome. 55¢ means you risk 55¢ to win 45¢ profit (pays out $1 total).
House cutThe platform's profit margin. 0% = no cut (rare, great deal). 2–3% = normal. The lower the better for you.
Close-game filterThe top slider flags real contests and skips blowouts. It flags any game where the market's most-likely result is at or below your % — so the lopsided favorites (Spain, Brazil, etc.) drop off, and the toss-ups stay. Lower = only true coin-flips; higher = also include moderate favorites. A game also flags if your own view differs from the market by 7%+.
Per-game sliderInside a game, drag toward the team you think will win. If your view is off from Fair % by at least the edge filter, the game gets flagged. Example: Fair % says Iran 52%, you think 65% → a 13pt gap flags Iran ML.
