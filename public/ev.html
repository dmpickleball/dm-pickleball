House cutThe platform's profit margin. 0% = no cut (rare, great deal). 2–3% = normal. The lower the better for you.
Edge filterThe top slider flags a game two ways at once: if any market is cheaply priced (small house cut) OR your view differs from the market enough. Lower = looser (more games); higher = stricter (only the best prices / biggest disagreements). "Show flagged only" hides the rest.
