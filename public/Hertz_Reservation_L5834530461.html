US Dream Car Rental Requirements

Hertz Dream Cars® provides a selection of ultra-high end vehicles available for customers.

 Special rules apply for the rental of a Dream Cars®

  • Renter and any additional drivers must be at least 25 years old
  • ID requirements: 
    • Valid Driver’s License
    • Credit card (debit cards not accepted at time of rental)
    • Proof of auto insurance or secondary Government issued ID
  • If identification is not accepted, Hertz may offer an alternative vehicle model.

*Driver’s identification will be photocopied, sealed, secured and shredded when the Dream Cars vehicle is returned. Rental hours: 6:00am – 12am.



DESTINATION SPECIFIC TERMS
  • At the time of pick-up you will need to present a valid driver’s licence, a passport or ID card, and the credit or debit card that was used for the booking online. This MUST be presented by the person in whose name the vehicle was booked.
  • For some Premium car groups you may be required to present one or two credit cards. Details can be found in the Forms of Payment information.
  • Debit cards are only accepted for the following vehicle groups in Italy: (A) Fiat 500 or similar, (B) Fiat Panda or similar, (C) Lancia Y or similar.
  • Hertz will reserve credit (if paying with a credit card) or take a pre-authorised deposit (if paying with a debit card) to cover the estimated charges arising from your rental, including a full tank of fuel. The reserved credit/deposit will be released on final calculation and payment of your rental charges on return (minus any charges). Please check with your card issuer on when your deposit will appear in your account (est. 4-7 days, but it could take up to 30 days).
  • At the time of rental, credit or charge cards must have available credit, and acceptable debit/check cards must have available funds, sufficient to pay the estimated amount of the rental charges plus the credit reserved or deposit pre-authorised by Hertz.
  • You may find a slight variation between the exchange rate you receive when you collect your car compared to when you return it. The exchange rate is given to you by your bank, so please refer to your bank’s policy for more information.
NATIONAL VEHICLE REGISTRY
  • It is mandatory to pass to the National Vehicle Registry in the Department of Motor Vehicles the names of those using a vehicle for more than 30 days (update of art 94, paragraph 4-bis of the Highway Code and 247-bis, paragraph 2, letter b) of the Presidential Decree # 495/1992), making it compulsory for the user to communicate the relevant details to the National Vehicle Registry. Hertz will communicate your details to the National Vehicle Registry provided delegation is signed at the rental counter.
SNOW CHAINS OR WINTER TYRES
  • From 15 Nov to 15 Apr, many Italian cities are required by law to restrict the circulation to only vehicles equipped with snow chains or winter tyres. For more information visit www.poliziadistato.it
  • To comply with the regulations, at locations subject to this requirement in specific Italian regions, vehicles are equipped with snow chains. The price is included in the rental charges under Winterisation Fee. Please check the Hertz Terms and Conditions for more information.



Requirements for Collection

  • The named driver must present a valid driver’s licence and at least one credit or debit card in his/her name, which must be the one used for the online booking.
  • When you collect the vehicle we will block a reservation amount on your credit card to cover us for all unpaid rental and optional extra charges plus a full tank of fuel/recharge (the  minimum amount will be €200, if you have elected to pay at the location the amount blocked on your card will equal the total estimated rental charges for the rental period ). The reserved amount will not be accessible by you during your rental. We will release the reservation amount after final calculation and payment of your rental charges on return (minus any charges). However, it may take up to 30 days to show in your account due to your card issuer’s processes. For more information please see here. Full details can be found in the Forms of Payment information.
